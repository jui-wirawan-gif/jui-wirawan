Salty snacks category review for PepsiCo

Circana, SydneyFeb–Apr 2026My presentationUnify+ · Excel · PowerPoint
Question
Where is growth in Australian salty snacks really coming from, and what should PepsiCo do about it?
Approach
Pulled and reconciled scan (Market Read) and shopper panel data in Circana's Unify+ platform, then built a 10–12 slide review told in three acts, ending in recommendations.
So what
A flat headline number was hiding a category moving in different directions by retailer and by segment. The review turned that into product recommendations for PepsiCo and was presented to 12 colleagues, including senior consultants.

What I learned: lead with the insight, not the data. Coaching from senior consultants pushed me to re-cut the data until every slide had a clear "so what".

Business analytics · Predictive modelling

When do financial advisers recommend NEOS?

COMM3501Team of 5R · Random Forest · XGBoost
Question
Life insurer NEOS is recommended in only 12.3% of advised cases. Which customer, product and adviser factors drive that recommendation?
Approach
Cleaned 165,000 adviser records, explored patterns by commission structure, age and product, then compared four classification models. Random Forest was kept as a decision-support model: 94.9% AUC, 91.6% balanced accuracy.
So what
Three levers to win more recommendations: a fee-for-service tier for the 61% of the market NEOS is locked out of today, a "consider adding cover" prompt in the quoting workflow (bundles lift NEOS's odds by about 40%), and targeted adviser outreach in four underperforming states.

My role: helped build the models in R with the team, then led the recommendations, including the product concept mockups.

Business analytics · Text analytics

What Insta360 users struggle with after filming

INFS3830 Social Media AnalyticsIndividualSAS Viya Text Analytics
Question
Insta360 camera owners post thousands of comments about their workflow online. Where does the experience break down?
Approach
Analysed 4,713 user posts in SAS Viya. Started from predefined concepts and sentiment topics, then wrote my own concept rules and categories, using Boolean, proximity and exclusion logic, to isolate editing problems and requests for help.
So what
The pain is in editing, not the camera: playback and export in the Studio app, with crashes reported on Intel machines. I recommended targeted software fixes, a compatibility project with Intel, and an in-app tutorial library starting with basic editing.
Corporate finance · M&A valuation

Should Tata Motors buy Harman at market price?

FINS3625 Applied Corporate FinanceIndividualExcel · DCF · Monte Carlo
Question
Harman's market value is US$5.2bn. How much can Tata pay before the deal destroys value for its shareholders?
Approach
Re-levered Harman's beta to revalue it as a standalone business, valued the expected synergies at Tata's 13.95% cost of capital (their risk follows Tata's car business, not Harman's), then ran 1,000 Monte Carlo trials on the least certain input, the Year 3 synergy forecast.
So what
The maximum defensible price is US$3.8bn. Paying market value would destroy about US$1.45bn, and every one of the 1,000 trials came out negative. Recommendation: bid no higher than US$3.8bn, push for an earn-out tied to realised synergies, or walk away.

Case inputs such as the synergy forecast came from the course brief.
